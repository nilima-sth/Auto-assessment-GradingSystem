Q1: Explain the Difference Between e-Government and e-Governance.
A1: Difference Between e-Government and e-Governance: e-Government focuses on delivering public services electronically, while e-Governance also includes the wider use of digital technologies to improve participation, transparency, and government processes. (10)

Q2: What is a digital signature?
A2: A digital signature is a cryptographic mechanism used to verify the authenticity and integrity of a digital message or document. (5)
